TRAITS & RARITIES

Each 100% unique and hand-drawn Fedwas Croc collectible is programmatically and randomly generated from over 120 different traits. A Fedwas Croc has its own set of unique features that emerge from the following trait options:

  • Skin
  • Background
  • Eyes & Eyewear
  • Clothing
  • Headwear
  • Accesories

Each trait has a rarity index that makes some Fedwas more mind-blowing than others.

While there are a few extra unique ones, all Fedwas are beautiful reptilian specimens.

FAQ's

What are Fedwas?

The Fedwas are the first limited collection of 10,000 unique NFT tokens, living on the Ethereum blockchain. Fedwas owners roam in their swamp and roam in numerous adventures..

When will it drop?

Fedwas will drop in late October. Please check our Discord and Twitter channels for the latest updates.

How can I get a Croc?

You can acquire a Fedwas Croc by connecting your Ethereum wallet (such as Metamask) to our website and minting your own Croc upon launch. Remember, amounts are limited!

What is the price of each croc?

Each Fedwas Croc costs 0.04 ETH + gas.

Will there be a max mint per transaction?

Yes. You’re able to mint up to 20 Fedwas per transaction.

Where can I see my collection of ?

You’ll be able to find your collection of by connecting your Ethereum/MetaMask wallet to OpenSea.

What benefits will come from owning a croc?

Fedwas Croc owners are members of our community—The Swamp. The Swamp offers rewards, royalties, airdrops, and prizes to all owners.

Are there collaborations planned?

Yes! We’re already in talks with well-known crypto artists and collections that will collaborate with us on exclusive pieces for our community.

What are the fees on Opensea?

Listing a Fedwas Croc on OpenSea doesn’t have a cost, other than the gas fees required in order to list one.

How do I access the private community?

You’ll need to verify the ownership of your Fedwas within our Discord in order to gain access to our exclusive secret underwater lair.
